# Cosmos route v1.8

## Route identity
Cosmos is not a fixed-stat fighter. The route is built around changing the meaning of the same enemy turn by changing form through ACT.

### Luna Mode
- Defensive / mercy form.
- During enemy turns, `Z / Enter / Space` emits a short-range purification pulse.
- The pulse automatically clears a small number of nearby Chaos projectiles instead of requiring pixel-perfect aiming.
- ACT can expand the pulse for the next round.

### Corona Mode
- Direct breakthrough form.
- Hold a direction and press `Z / Enter / Space` to burst-dash in that direction.
- The dash has a brief invulnerability window and breaks Chaos projectiles along its path.
- It is not a passive ATK buff: the player actively cuts through an attack lane to create space.

### Eclipse Mode
- Unlocked in the Chaos Ultraman battle.
- `Z / Enter / Space` selects and cuts the nearest high-value Chaos structure in range.
- It is intended for separating dangerous Chaos components rather than simply clearing everything.

### Miracle Luna
- Scripted final-battle state.
- The final objective changes from reducing HP to reaching `HEART 100`.
- Luna pulse converts nearby hostility into a response that can reach Chaos Header.
- TEAM EYES shoots down attacks that would interrupt the purification route.
- Lidorias / Mogurudon / Bolgils periodically turn a hostile Chaos projectile into a receptive heart fragment rather than deleting it.

## Encounters

### 1. Chaos Lidorias
- Formal Cosmos-route opener.
- Teaches ACT form switching.
- Normal defeat remains possible, but Luna can build CALM and use Full Moon Rect for MERCY.

### 2. Chaos Ultraman / Chaos Ultraman Calamity
- `COPY` gauge rises if the player lets the imitation study one approach for too long.
- High COPY both increases enemy-turn pressure and reduces the player's direct damage efficiency.
- Switching forms, Luna purification, Corona breakthrough, Eclipse precision cuts and selected skills reduce COPY.
- Multiple attack families: mirror crescents, order nodes, impedance walls and prominence sweeps.
- Mid-battle cinematic unlocks Eclipse, then the apparent defeat of the copy is reversed when Chaos light reconstructs it as Calamity.

### 3. Chaos Header · Chaos Darkness
- Early phase deliberately allows Luna / Corona / Eclipse combat, showing that every escalation becomes another thing Chaos learns.
- Mid-battle collapse changes the premise: Musashi rejects destruction as the only answer and Miracle Luna begins.
- Multiple final attack families: hatred volleys, broken halos with readable safe gaps, assimilation lines, heart fragments and sweeping walls.
- The final battle is not won by reaching 0 HP. Chaos Darkness is held at 1 HP after the understanding phase begins.
- `HEART 100` unlocks Luna Final. The result is `mercy`, transforming the black manifestation into Chaos Header 0.
