# Original Route v2.5.1 — Zetton / Greeza combat-language pass

This pass intentionally touches only the first two original-route encounters. It does not redesign Grand King, Five King, or Belial.

## Zetton

### Phase A — directional defence

The guard sequence is authored rather than randomly generated. Four score variants define exact warning order and timing. Projectile speed is calculated from the actual edge-to-centre distance, so left/right and up/down attacks share the same cue-to-impact timing even on a wide arena.

The first form also changes rules without changing model: two enemy turns lock the player to a four-direction guard, then the third turn releases the lock and becomes a free-movement teleport-lane / target-fireball round.

Yellow reverse cues appear only after normal directional beats have already established the base grammar.

### Phase B — Hyper Zetton Imago

Hyper Zetton remains free-movement and cycles three authored phrases:

1. afterimage teleport lanes;
2. snapshot fireball lock zones;
3. deliberate rush/fireball alternation.

There is no side/timing RNG in these phrases. If a large beam has been absorbed, the returned beam is placed in a separate late beat rather than stacked on top of another lethal pattern.

## Greeza

### First form — thunder smash

Only the strike position is random. The timeline is fixed. A square + circle + exclamation mark appears around the exact impact zone before lightning smashes directly into that patch. Major strikes do not overlap.

### Second form — four different enemy turns

1. **Vortex field** — several drifting vortex hazards occupy the arena for the whole turn. No unrelated projectile family is mixed into this round.
2. **Greeza evil sound** — a Greeza representative appears at arena centre. Expanding rings are warned by colour only about 0.9 s before release. Blue rings punish movement; orange rings punish standing still.
3. **Dark lightning** — the first-form thunder language returns with shorter warnings and authored staggered double strikes.
4. **Double spiral** — paired void emitters at opposite arena edges fire two intertwined sinusoidal energy strands. Horizontal and vertical versions occur as separate readable beats.

### Final form — alternating signature rounds

- **Wave cannon turn** — individual diagonal beams originate at the top-centre chest aperture and target authored lanes. They are telegraphed as lines from emitter to impact direction; they are not generic full-screen wall bands.
- **Light-bullet rain turn** — repeated fan rows fire from the same chest source. Each row deliberately omits two adjacent lanes. The safe corridor moves one lane at a time so a no-hit route remains physically reachable.
