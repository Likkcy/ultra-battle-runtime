# Cosmos Route v2.1 Rebuild

## Chaos Lidorias
- Dedicated protection-area intro cinematic.
- CALM now has four narrative recognition milestones at ~28/55/82/100.
- Luna gains a Lidorias-specific “叫它的名字” ACT.
- Full Moon Rect now launches a dedicated purification/return cinematic rather than the generic MERCY handler.

## Chaos Ultraman
- Expanded from two combat phases to three: COPY -> ECLIPSE BREAK -> CALAMITY.
- Eclipse awakening is now its own playable phase.
- Calamity only appears after a later apparent separation/defeat and reconstitutes with restored HP/COPY.

## Chaos Darkness
- Expanded from two phases to three: HATRED -> NO ANSWER -> MIRACLE LUNA.
- HEART gauge stays hidden until the fight meaning actually changes.
- Ordinary damage in Miracle Luna no longer reduces HP and instead reduces HEART.
- Converted HEART fragments are collectible objects.
- HEART 25/50/75/100 each have distinct story/visual beats.
- Luna Final has a substantially longer shell-peeling / Chaos Header 0 ending.

No route BGM was added in this build; soundtrack slots remain for the later music pass.
