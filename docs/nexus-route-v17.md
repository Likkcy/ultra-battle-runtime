# Nexus Route v1.7

## Route identity

The Nexus route is built around **life as a combat resource** and **light as an inherited bond**. The route keeps the existing per-round life drain and damage-to-life recovery system from the Pedoleon fight, then changes the meaning of the enemy turn and the player controls in the two later boss fights.

### 1. Pedoleon

The existing opening encounter remains the route tutorial: Nexus loses life each completed enemy round, damage dealt can recover life, and the player must sever feeding cells instead of only dodging. Meta Field increases both danger and recovery potential.

### 2. Dark Mephisto Zwei — Junis Blue

- Starts directly in Junis Blue after a Dark Field G entry cinematic.
- `DARK DRAIN` measures how much light the enemy has stolen. High drain makes frontal damage less effective.
- During the enemy turn, `Z / Enter / Space` fires a small blue shot. It can destroy red-eye marks, absorption orbs and other attack objects.
- The idea is inspired by the *principle* of an active enemy-turn shooter: attacks are not merely walls to dodge, but tactical objects the player may decide to destroy.
- The attack language includes spear fans, red-eye targets, drain orbs, claw sweeps and cross-beams.
- Final phase: Mizorogi Shinya returns as Dark Mephisto and physically restrains one side of Zwei. He does not auto-win the battle; he creates cleaner shot windows and lowers the drain pressure.

### 3. Dark Zagi — four inherited forms

The fight has four authored phases and the control rule changes with each form.

1. **Anphans / Komon** — survive and read. `Z` emits a short pulse that neutralizes a nearby simple dark projectile. Zagi uses orb fans, claw sweeps, aimed needles and expanding shock rings.
2. **Junis / memory of Jun Himeya** — the fight becomes close-range and confrontational. `Z` is a timed parry against Zagi rushes. Beam sweeps, crosses and rush chains force the player to stand their ground.
3. **Junis Blue / memory of Ren Senjyu** — speed and active shooting return. `Z` fires at dark nodes while the arena adds needle rain, teleport crosses and homing orbs.
4. **Ultraman Noa / the bond completed** — per-round life drain stops. The player can return specific heavy red spheres, while Zagi uses large lightning, shock rings and cosmic sweeps with less clutter but much larger scale.

## Story direction

The finale is not a Tiga-style miracle and not a Ginga-style friend support phase. The transformations are presented as combat memory carried forward: Himeya's persistence, Ren's decision to keep running, Riko's final protective choice, Nagi, Mizorogi, Night Raider and the people who finally witness and trust the giant. The final idea is simple: no single person owns the light. Each person keeps it from ending in their hands.

`NEXUS` is therefore both the route gauge and the dramatic thesis: **a bond / a link / inherited light**.
