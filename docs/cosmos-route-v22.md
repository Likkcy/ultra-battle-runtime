# Cosmos Route v2.2 — Continuity / Set-piece Upgrade

v2.2 continues directly from the v2.1 three-battle structure. It does **not** replace the combat skeleton and does not add Cosmos BGM. The focus is to make the three encounters feel like one accumulating Chaos story and to turn several text-only transitions into visible set-pieces.

## Route continuity

The canonical story order remains:

```text
Chaos Lidorias — MERCY
        ↓
Chaos Ultraman — VICTORY
        ↓
Chaos Darkness — MIRACLE LUNA / HEART / MERCY
```

UBR does **not** advance that route by itself. The card / UBR Bridge remains the story source of truth and selects the next fixed encounter through `route + battleId`. The runtime only executes the requested registered encounter and returns its battle result.

## 1. Chaos Lidorias — the first memory

The v2.1 CALM / recognition battle remains intact. Its ending now functions as the first explicit memory that Chaos carries into later encounters:

- Cosmos has the ability to attack, but stops once Lidorias returns.
- Chaos therefore observes a decision it does not understand: strength deliberately not used as violence.
- The later bosses reference this moment instead of behaving like unrelated Chaos organisms.

## 2. Chaos Ultraman — learning choice

The opening now shows a fragment of the Lidorias encounter before the copy finishes forming.

Chaos is no longer framed as only copying Cosmos's body and techniques. It is trying to learn **how Cosmos chooses between restraint, attack and separation**.

The Calamity transition is rebuilt as a staged reconstruction:

1. Eclipse separates the copy structures.
2. The fragments remain suspended instead of simply dying.
3. Learned answers appear inside them: Lidorias restraint, Luna pacification, Corona breakthrough, Eclipse separation.
4. Chaos discards the reasons behind those choices and keeps only their combat utility.
5. The fragments collapse inward and rebuild into Chaos Ultraman Calamity.

Calamity also receives a distinct live battle silhouette: spined back shell, altered head, blade-like arms and a hotter core. It should read as a self-authored combat organism, not merely the previous copy tinted red.

## 3. Chaos Darkness — everything we taught it

The final battle begins with a visible archive of the previous encounters. Chaos Darkness is the accumulation of what Chaos learned from monsters, Cosmos and human firepower.

### NO ANSWER

The failed escalation is no longer compressed into one explanation. It unfolds in four beats:

- **Luna:** pacification is anticipated and replaced from behind.
- **Corona:** the breakthrough route is predicted and sealed.
- **Eclipse:** separation becomes a template for new reconstruction.
- **TEAM EYES:** human fire is recorded into the hostile attack array.

The point of the phase is not that Cosmos lacks a stronger form. It is that every new answer delivered as a combat answer becomes another weapon Chaos can learn.

### MIRACLE LUNA

The awakening keeps the v2.1 HEART rules but adds a dedicated visual statement: Cosmos lowers one fist, lowers the other, then opens both hands.

This is not helplessness. Cosmos still has power; the posture shows the deliberate decision to stop pointing that power at Chaos.

### Chaos Header 0

The final shell removal is split into three visible beats:

1. Shoulder shell cracks while Cosmos refuses to increase light pressure.
2. Chest shell falls and the orange core begins turning gold.
3. The last black pieces loosen by themselves rather than being destroyed.

The final state remains Chaos Header 0: the ending is a choice and release, not a kill animation.

## Soundtrack

No Cosmos route BGM is added in v2.2. Music integration remains deferred until the soundtrack material is ready.
